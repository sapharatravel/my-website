Frequently asked. Honestly answered.

Why not just book it myself?

You can. Booking with us costs no more: there's no service fee, and when we compare the same room, dates and cancellation terms, we often find it for less. The difference is what comes with it. We check what booking sites don't tell you, like the food and alcohol situation, pass your requests to the hotel before you arrive, and stay with you on WhatsApp if anything goes wrong while you're away.

What happens after I send a message?

We'll get back to you as soon as we can, with options. Bigger trips can take a few days.

Can you check halal food where we're going?

Most of our trips are to Muslim-majority countries, where it rarely comes up. For places like Europe or Japan, we'll check the hotel's food and alcohol situation and tell you plainly.

Are all your hotels halal or alcohol-free?

No. We check each hotel and tell you plainly what it offers.

Is my booking ATOL protected?

Flights and flight-inclusive packages we book are ATOL protected by the ATOL holder we book them with, and you'll receive an ATOL Certificate. Hotel-only bookings and other services without a flight aren't covered by ATOL.
