Got a question? We're happy to help.

Why not just book it myself?

You can book online yourself. We don't charge a service fee, and when we compare the same room, dates and cancellation terms, we often find it for less. We can also check hotel details, pass on your requests and stay in touch on WhatsApp if something goes wrong while you're away.

What happens after I send a message?

We'll get back to you with some options. Bigger trips can take a few days to put together.

Can you check halal food where we're going?

Tell us what you need and where you're going. We'll check the hotel's food and alcohol arrangements and explain what we find.

Are all your hotels halal or alcohol-free?

No. We check each hotel and tell you plainly what it offers, so you can decide whether it works for you.

Is my booking ATOL protected?

Flights and flight-inclusive packages we book are ATOL protected by the ATOL holder we book them with, and you'll receive an ATOL Certificate. Hotel-only bookings and other services without a flight aren't covered by ATOL.
